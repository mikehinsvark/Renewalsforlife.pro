02 / A DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVE

Same skill. Same effort.
A different income structure.

A sale can end with a payment.
Or begin a relationship that creates
opportunities for ongoing income.

THE TRANSACTIONAL MODEL↻

Every year,
a fresh starting line.

Earn from the work you do now. When new production stops, the income from that production stops too.

Work → payment → repeatONE-TIME INCOME
THE RENEWAL MODEL∞

Every year,
another layer to build on.

New business adds to income from eligible renewing clients. Existing relationships can keep creating value.

Build → retain → build againLAYERED INCOME

Conceptual comparison. Renewal payments depend on the product, client retention, and contract terms.
